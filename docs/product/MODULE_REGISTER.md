# Module Register

## M01 — No Lost Referral™

### Problem
Referrals can disappear between receipt, contact, booking, waiting list, client response and allocation.

### Required functions
- register each referral;
- source and received timestamp;
- responsible owner;
- contact status;
- contact attempts;
- next action;
- due date/time;
- referral value estimate;
- waiting status;
- booked status;
- declined/not suitable status;
- transfer and acceptance of ownership;
- escalation for no action;
- lost-referral reason capture;
- conversion reporting.

### Key metric
**Referral leakage value avoided / revenue recovered.**

---

## M02 — Reception Flow & Follow-up

### Problem
Reception loses time repeating calls, searching for context and remembering who needs follow-up.

### Required functions
- callback queue;
- follow-up queue;
- priority;
- standard status codes;
- next action;
- notes limited to operational context;
- contact-attempt history;
- overdue escalation;
- team workload view.

### Key metric
**Minutes/hours saved and unresolved contacts reduced.**

---

## M03 — Appointment Leakage & Refill

### Problem
Late cancellations and no-shows create unused capacity and lost revenue.

### Required functions
- cancellation capture;
- reason category;
- vacancy window;
- waitlist candidate matching;
- contact attempts;
- refill result;
- recovered appointment value;
- repeated cancellation/no-show pattern reporting.

### Key metric
**Appointment revenue recovered.**

---

## M04 — No-Lost-Responsibility / Work Ownership

### Problem
Tasks remain unfinished because ownership is unclear.

### Required functions
- every action has one current owner;
- due time/date;
- transfer requires recipient acceptance;
- overdue escalation;
- close reason;
- reopen function;
- immutable action history.

### Key metric
**Reduction in unowned and overdue operational work.**

---

## M05 — Staff Time Waste & Duplication Detection

### Problem
Staff repeat tasks, enter the same information twice, search for missing details, perform unnecessary administration or work outside the appropriate role.

### Required functions
- waste-event logging;
- category: duplicate work / rework / searching / waiting / unnecessary approval / wrong-role work / avoidable manual entry;
- estimated minutes;
- staff role;
- recurrence;
- root cause;
- corrective action;
- before/after comparison.

### Key metric
**Verified staff hours released and labour-value savings.**

---

## M06 — Leave, Handover & Absence Continuity

### Problem
Work is dropped, repeated or delayed when a staff member is absent.

### Required functions
- pre-leave handover queue;
- work requiring cover;
- temporary owner;
- acceptance;
- return-from-leave briefing;
- unexpected absence reassignment;
- unaccepted handover escalation.

### Key metric
**Lost/repeated work prevented during absence.**

---

## M07 — Capacity & Utilisation

### Problem
Paid staff and appointment capacity can be underused while demand exists elsewhere.

### Required functions
- available appointment capacity;
- filled/unfilled capacity;
- waitlist demand;
- referral demand;
- cancellation capacity;
- staff workload range;
- avoidable idle-capacity events;
- utilisation trend.

### Key metric
**Recoverable and recovered productive capacity.**

---

## M08 — Recurring Cost / Supplier Waste Review

### Problem
Duplicate subscriptions, unused services and poor purchasing decisions quietly consume money.

### Required functions
- recurring expense register;
- owner;
- service purpose;
- renewal date;
- usage/relevance review;
- duplicate flag;
- keep/cancel/renegotiate decision;
- implemented saving;
- evidence.

### Key metric
**Annual recurring cost removed or reduced.**

---

## M09 — Systemic Pattern, Waste & Prevention Command

### Problem
Individual events are fixed but the underlying pattern keeps recurring.

### Required functions
- group repeated waste events;
- identify common causes;
- rank by cost;
- assign prevention action;
- track intervention;
- compare before and after;
- prevent double-counting of savings.

### Key metric
**Recurring loss permanently reduced.**

---

## M10 — Verified Savings Ledger & Director Dashboard

### Problem
Savings claims are meaningless if they are not traceable.

### Required functions
- potential saving;
- calculation method;
- baseline;
- intervention;
- actual result;
- evidence;
- verification status;
- verifier;
- confidence/quality field;
- monthly/quarterly/yearly totals;
- separation of recovered revenue, avoided cost and released staff time.

### Key metric
**Verified annualised business benefit.**
