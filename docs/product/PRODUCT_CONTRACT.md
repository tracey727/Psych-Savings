# Product Contract — Practice Savings & Revenue Protection Command

## 1. Product objective

Create one operational command system for Irene's psychology practice that identifies and prevents money loss caused by operational leakage.

The system must make invisible loss visible. It must show:
- where a referral was lost;
- which contact attempt failed;
- which appointment slot went unused;
- which staff task was repeated;
- which responsibility had no owner;
- which handover failed;
- where senior staff are doing lower-level work;
- where staff hours are being consumed without business value;
- where recurring operating costs are unnecessary;
- what corrective action was taken;
- what financial value was recovered or saved.

## 2. Non-negotiable scope

### Included
- referral intake and conversion workflow;
- reception follow-up;
- appointment cancellation/no-show recovery;
- waiting-list refill;
- staff task ownership;
- handovers and leave cover;
- workload visibility;
- repeated/duplicated administrative work;
- capacity utilisation;
- recurring cost review;
- operational waste pattern detection;
- action escalation;
- savings calculation and verification;
- director-level reporting.

### Excluded from the core product
- client personal budgeting;
- therapy content;
- clinical notes;
- diagnosis;
- treatment decisions;
- autonomous clinical risk decisions;
- payroll processing;
- accounting ledger replacement;
- full practice-management-system replacement.

The system may integrate with approved business systems later, but Phase 1 must remain capable of operating as a standalone operational-control layer.

## 3. Primary users

- Practice owner/director: Irene
- Authorised practice manager/operations lead
- Reception/administration staff
- Authorised clinicians for only the operational actions relevant to them
- Technical administrator, separated from business decision rights

## 4. Core business promise

No important referral, callback, appointment recovery action, handover or operational responsibility should disappear merely because a staff member forgets, is absent, becomes overloaded or assumes someone else is handling it.

## 5. Decision principles

1. Every money-relevant item has an owner.
2. Every owner has a due time or next action.
3. Every overdue item becomes visible.
4. High-value leakage escalates faster.
5. Closed work requires evidence or an outcome reason.
6. Savings are never counted merely because the system suggested them.
7. Management can see patterns without reading clinical information.
8. Clinical information is minimised and segregated.
9. The system records who changed material business data.
10. Nothing is silently deleted from the operational history.

## 6. Operating states

### Green
Normal. Item has a valid owner, current action and deadline.

### Amber / Yellow
Attention required. Deadline approaching, response outstanding, repeat failure occurring or value at risk.

### Red
Revenue or operational loss is likely/occurring; required action is overdue or ownership has failed.

### Recovery
Corrective action is actively underway after a Red event.

## 7. Savings states

### Potential
A possible saving/revenue recovery has been identified.

### Approved
An authorised manager accepts the intervention.

### Implemented
The intervention was carried out.

### Measured
Actual financial or time impact has been calculated.

### Verified
Evidence supports the claimed saving/recovery.

## 8. Success criteria

The system is successful when it can demonstrate, with evidence:
- reduction in lost referrals;
- higher referral-to-booking conversion;
- faster first-contact times;
- more cancelled appointments refilled;
- fewer unowned or overdue tasks;
- fewer duplicate staff actions;
- reduced avoidable admin time;
- improved clinician/room/appointment utilisation where applicable;
- recurring cost reductions;
- a verified savings total Irene can audit.

## 9. Ownership principle

The product is designed as an Irene-practice operational module that can sit within the wider GENEVIEVE App™ ecosystem while preserving clear module boundaries, permissions and data ownership.
